Finding the cells hidden in the crowd

Rare cells can reveal immune defenses and treatment resistance, yet common gene-selection methods may overlook signals concentrated in very few cells. Losing these signals leaves important biology hidden. PalmaClust uses the Palma ratio, a tail-sensitive inequality measure, to prioritize rare-cell marker genes and combine complementary views for discovery.

An overlooked clue in the tumor

Immunotherapy can transform cancer care, but many patients do not benefit, and familiar biomarkers leave much of this variation unexplained. Overlooked immune populations may offer additional clues. GammaDelta investigates whether pretreatment tumor γδ T-cell abundance is associated with immunotherapy response and survival, motivating more informative immune profiling and prospective validation.

ACM-BCB · ParBio2019

Should a biological question have to wait for a bigger computer?

Comparing protein sequences helps reveal shared functions, but the number of comparisons grows rapidly as collections expand. Long runtimes and the burden of managing computing infrastructure can put large analyses out of reach. This project explores serverless parallel computing to make large-scale sequence comparison faster and more accessible to researchers.
